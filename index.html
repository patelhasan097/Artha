Peter Lynch Valuation
How this works

Peter Lynch's Fair Value formula

From Peter Lynch's book One Up On Wall Street — a quick gut-check on whether a growth stock's price makes sense next to its growth.

(EPS Growth % + Dividend Yield %) ÷ P/E Ratio

Below 1.0 — the stock's price has run ahead of its growth. Overvalued.

1.0 to 1.5 — price and growth are roughly in balance. Fairly valued.

Above 1.5 — growth is outpacing what the price reflects. Undervalued.

This is a quick screening tool, not investment advice — always look at the full picture before deciding.
